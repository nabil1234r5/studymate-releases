# StudyMate

A study app for university students: timetable, tasks, notes, flashcards and an AI tutor in one place. Free, with no ads and no tracking.

## Get it

- **Android:** [Download StudyMate.apk](https://github.com/nabil1234r5/studymate-releases/releases/latest/download/StudyMate.apk)
- **iPhone, laptop or any phone:** use it in your browser at https://studymate-app.expo.app
- **More about the app:** https://studymate-app.expo.app/download.html

## Installing on Android

1. Tap the download link above and wait for the file to finish.
2. Open the downloaded file. Your phone asks to allow installing from this source. Allow it, then tap Install.
3. If Play Protect shows a warning, choose "Install anyway". It appears because StudyMate is not on the Play Store yet.

To update, download the newest file and install it over the old one. Your data stays.

## What it does

- Shows today's classes, deadlines and study sessions.
- Reads your timetable from a photo, and lets you check it before saving.
- Answers questions step by step with an AI tutor, including photos of problems.
- Turns a note into a summary, flashcards or a quiz.
- Works offline on Android. Only the AI and backup need internet.
- Backs up your work to an optional free account, so a new phone can restore it.
- English and French, light and dark.
